# Product discipline

Do not copy another brand. Copy the discipline: few tokens, a dense tool, air in the story, real icons, empty states as product.

## Linear

- Space: 4 8 12 16 24 32
- Radius: 6 8 12 — modest
- App body ~13–14, not a marketing 16
- Almost monochrome, one accent that is theirs
- Issue rows are short; the first screen holds a lot of meaning
- Empty: short line, icon, one create action
- Motion 180–280ms, no bounce
- Lesson: a product is a tool, not a landing page

## Stripe

- App space: 0 2 4 8 16 24 32 48
- Radius 8 12 16, no blobs
- Strong display↔body jump
- Dashboard: gray surfaces, 1px border, almost no shadow
- Forms: label above the field, a clear error, never placeholder-as-label
- Docs and app are visually related, not identical
- Lesson: money and trust = contrast, calm, zero costume

## Notion

- Space: 4 8 12 16 20 24 32 48
- Radius: 4 8 12 pill
- Editor is airy; sidebar and menus are dense
- Page icons are user content. Chrome uses ordinary glyphs
- Empty workspace explains what the page is and offers a template
- Lesson: two densities in one product

## Figma

- Space: 4 8 12 16 24 48
- Radius: 2 6 8 — sharper
- Chrome is dense; the canvas is empty on purpose (that is the work surface)
- Own sans, not “just Inter”
- Panels: 1px rules, small captions, 16 icons with a larger hit area
- Lesson: radius and type shrink when people work for hours

## Apple products

- System controls, little custom chrome
- One accent
- Settings: grouped list, 16/20 margins, SF, SF Symbols
- Empty in Photos / Files: a short line plus how to add
- Glass only on chrome; content stays itself

## Google products

- Gmail / Drive: high density, one FAB for create
- Selected items use a colored container, not only bold text
- Material Symbols, fill on the active tab
- Empty inbox: “No mail” plus a next action

## Fluent

- 4 px base; 2 / 6 / 10 so icons sit on the grid
- Default radius **4**, large 8–12
- Segoe, body 14/20
- Touch 44, Android 48
- Lesson: enterprise can be warm without gradients and without 16 px radius on everything

## Carbon

- 2x grid, space 2…160
- Productive type vs expressive type
- Density is a value: data, not mood
- Plex is a face, not a prompt font
- Lesson: tokens and grid beat taste

## Take into any project

1. Few radii, few shadows, a lot of alignment
2. One icon family
3. App ≠ landing
4. Empty is written as product
5. Accent is rare
6. Tables and lists scan; not everything is a card

## Leave behind

- Mesh gradients “like Stripe” (Stripe’s app does not do this)
- Bento as a substitute for information architecture
- Three feature cards as onboarding
- Dark purple SaaS “like Linear” — Linear is not a purple gradient
