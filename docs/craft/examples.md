# Examples

Patterns with a before / after. Apply them in the product’s language.

## 1. Gradient hero

Before: gradient text, slogan, emoji.  
After: a flat title that states the job (“Invoices go out in two minutes”).

## 2. Feature cards

Before: three columns, a tinted tile, emoji, identical copy.  
After: a column or a real list of objects (icon + title + one line).

## 3. Empty

Before: a blank box or “No data”.  
After: icon from the family, “No orders yet”, why they appear, “Create invoice”.

## 4. Space

Before: magic pixels off the 4/8 scale, one radius on everything.  
After: token steps; radius follows role (control / card / overlay).

## 5. Dense table

Before: cards with 24 padding, a 30 title, three KPIs on top.  
After: a toolbar ~40 tall, rows 36–40, one filter, numbers with “last 7 days”, filter-empty “Reset status”.

## 6. Icon

Before: emoji or `✓` / `🔒` as icons.  
After: one icon family, named if icon-only.

## 7. Color

Before: indigo glow, default violet.  
After: the product accent, no halo.

## 8. Wrong control

Before: a long invoice form in a modal, button “OK”, disabled until 12 fields are perfect.  
After: a page. Primary “Issue invoice” stays active; errors rise on the fields. Cancel is a back link.

## 9. Tab carousel

Before: 9 tabs, three visible, the rest behind a swipe.  
After: 4 tabs, or a segmented control plus “More” as a list.

## 10. Focus

Before: hover only.  
After: hover **and** a 3:1 focus ring.

## 11. Disabled submit

Before: a gray Save that does nothing.  
After: Save is active. On press, the first invalid field is focused and explained.

## 12. Overlay choice

Before: settings for a team of twenty fields inside a dialog.  
After: a settings page. A dialog remains only for “Remove member?”.
